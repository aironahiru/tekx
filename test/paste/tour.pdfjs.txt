A Tour of tekx
Mathematics that means something
Ada Lovelace
2 November 2026
Abstract
This document is written in tekx. It shows how prose, structure and math-
ematics read naturally in source form, and how one source yields typeset
pages, MathML, spoken mathematics and clean copy text.
1 Prose and mathematics
Let us begin with the golden ratio, φ = (1 + √5)/2. It satisfies
φ^2 = φ + 1 (1)
which a screen reader speaks as “phi squared equals phi plus 1”, and which
copies out of the PDF as φ² = φ + 1. Paste that back into a tekx file and you
get the same formula.1 Long words such as incomprehensibilities are hyphen-
ated and still copy as one word; see [1] and https://example.org.
Theorem 1 (Pythagoras). For a right triangle with legs a, b and hypotenuse c,
a^2 + b^2 = c^2
Proof. Arrange four copies of the triangle inside a square of side a + b. ∎
Principle 2 (Least surprise). A formula should read as it is spoken.
Note. Equation (1) determines φ up to sign, and Theorem 1 is older
than Pythagoras.
